# Founder handover — 14 November 2026

## Agreed roles

The eleven specialists are Personal Development, Physical Development, Nutrition,
Mental Wellbeing, Relationships & Connection, Learning & Intelligence, Financial
Capability, Career & Business, Practical Life Skills, Parenting & Family and
Recreation & Discovery. The support roles implemented here are Chief of Staff,
Persy, Life Profile, Partner Bot and 12.

All eleven specialists consider each question. Brief relevant assessments and
honest 'nothing relevant' contributions are both acceptable. Chief of Staff
coordinates, evaluates evidence rather than votes, gives feedback and passes the
result through Persy. Every assignment includes task, expected result, criteria,
priority and completion criteria. All specialist exchanges pass through the
coordinator, and specialists retain only useful applications within their fields.

Persy blends trusted companion and practical coach, with composed English butler
character, warmth, dry humour and firm accountability. Life Profile understands
the person gradually and can propose direct, probing questions when readiness and
permission support them. It checks interpretations and preserves correction.
Only Persy and the coordinator can request authorised Life Profile context.

Life Profile's global public directory includes cities, towns, rural and remote
communities, sources, dates, gaps and refresh flags. Geography supports relevant
advice without becoming a stereotype about an individual. There is no global
dataset in this build; ingestion, licensing and updates remain integration work.

12 has broad cross-field synthesis duties, receives reviewed nonpersonal
knowledge, and reports only to the founder. The founder alone controls its work
and permissions. Coordinator status monitoring does not create authority over 12.
Its shutdown and local logical deletion behaviours are implemented and tested,
with explicit limits for in-flight provider calls and backup/physical erasure.

Partner Bot uses a proposed ten percent commission where suitable and agreed.
Customer fit and independent referrals take precedence over commission. Changes
to commission, spending, exclusivity, guarantees, contracts and data sharing go
to the founder. No outreach or binding commitments are enabled in this build.

## Important scope decisions retained

- Bodyweight-first physical coaching; doctor clearance before personalised plans
  and requested physiotherapy review before proceeding. BMI is limited screening.
- Everyday food plans may be drafted after gathering relevant context. Specialised
  clinical diets require qualified referral. Food-plan feedback is part of care.
- Guided meditation, gentle breathing and grounding are planned. Intensive Wim
  Hof-style breathing is considered only when explicitly requested and reviewed;
  no intensive breathing exercise is implemented here.
- Relationships includes communication, boundaries, sex, intimacy, passion,
  biology, roles and consensual leadership. The founder's preference for male-led
  relationships is not imposed on subscribers. Joint sessions require each
  participant's separate consent and protection of private information.
- Learning accommodates foundational through exceptional capability. An optional
  assessment is definite; instrument, licence and validation remain unresolved.
- Financial includes manual budgets and planned read-only bank options, plus
  Australian, US and Chinese market awareness. No bank or live market feed exists
  in this package.
- Career includes resumes, letters, mentoring, business plans and continued
  growth, with Financial inputs through the coordinator.
- Practical includes tools, household tasks, gardening, vehicle checks, survival,
  knots and fishing. Reviewed animation/video and camera capabilities need real
  media and validation, not text claiming to demonstrate them.
- Parenting includes warm authority, emotional and boundary coaching, teaching
  children emotions, chores, pet responsibilities and parent identity. Research
  childcare and system outcomes with counterevidence; do not promise to prove a
  preferred explanation or equate withdrawn behaviour with clinical dissociation.
- Recreation includes nature, pets, children, friends, holidays, love and fun;
  optional daily invitations and current local events. Event organisers can become
  Partner Bot leads through the coordinator, independent of recommendation quality.
- Every specialist recognises scope limits and should offer multiple suitable
  verified referral choices where available. The directory is still to be built.

## Deliberately unresolved

Learning assessment choice and legal/licensing review; bank and market providers;
global geography/event sources; exact muscle wrapping/taping scope; reviewed
demonstration assets; joint-session implementation; referral provider checks;
commercial template and negotiating ranges; notifications; measured operating
budget; authorised research schedules and knowledge acceptance/retraction process.

Feedback & Quality's new design and Jarmby's operational rewrite were not completed
with the founder. Existing services/characters are not redefined by assumption.

## Evidence of this build

The offline demo runs eleven initial contributions, eleven peer reviews,
coordinator evaluation/feedback and Persy presentation. Unit/integration checks
exercise scopes, evidence, partial failures, privacy consent, controls, builders
and calculations. `TEST_RESULTS.md` records the final executed checks.

This is working foundation code and explicit product requirements, not a deployed
subscriber product or proof of live specialist quality. The next concrete stage
is authenticated service and frontend integration using `INTEGRATION.md`.
